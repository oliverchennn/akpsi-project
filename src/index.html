Meet your virtual stockroom

Explore Tarely’s grocery inventory concept with four independent, simulated weighing platforms.

  1. Adjust the weight. Drag a circular dial, use its arrow keys (0.1 kg), Page Up/Down (1 kg), Home/End, or enter a gross weight.
  2. Watch the inventory. The saved empty-bin tare is subtracted. Net weight at or below your threshold creates one alert per crossing.
  3. Try a busy afternoon. Choose a run time from 15 seconds to 2 minutes. Starting stock drops gradually and demo requests turn on automatically. Change the pace at any time, or pause and resume from any view.
  4. Approve, then deliver. In Demo requests, approve a top-up, then simulate delivery to add that quantity. Refill all or Reset demo stops the simulation.

Everything here is a simulation. This demo does not measure hardware accuracy or predict future stock. Changes stay in this tab and reset when you reload.
